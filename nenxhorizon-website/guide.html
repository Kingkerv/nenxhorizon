Chapter 01

Getting started 首途

NenXHorizon is three places, and you move between them as you progress.

Menu

Where you are born. Six character slots, all the rolling, all the customising. Nothing here is permanent until you enter the world.

World

Whale Island. Port town, forest, shore, and the sky spar arena. This is where you train, fight, hunt, fish, and live.

Exams

The Hunter Exam. Passing it is the gate to nen. Until you clear it, meditation gives you nothing but a bad feeling.

Order of operations Make a character → learn the combat system in the world → pass the exam → awaken nen → train the six principles → collect cards → push your stats to the cap → chase a master. That is the spine of the game.

Everything you see, the faces, the GUIs, the moon phases, the emote icons, is hand made. The rigs are R6. If something looks drawn rather than generated, that is because it was.

Chapter 02

Character creation 生誕

Six nameable slots. Six lives. Roll them until one feels like yours.

What you pick

Hair, skin, eyes and faces are chosen straight from the list. Traits are chosen too. This is the part of your character you are in control of, so take your time with it.

What you roll

Everything else is a spin, and spins are the currency of creation. Four things come out of the machine:

SpinWhat it rolls
GenderStraightforward. Reroll as many times as you like.
HeightYour height in centimetres. Small and tall builds feel genuinely different to play.
Face mark60% of the time you get a clean face. Marks are the rare outcome, not the default.
ClanYour bloodline. This is the one people grind for.

Clans

Bloodlines sit on a rarity table, and at the very bottom of it is Zoldyck at 0.05%. That is one in two thousand spins. People have burned a thousand clan spins and walked away without it. That is the point.

Spin economy Spins come from Jenny and from redeem codes. If you are hunting a specific clan, stack clan spins from codes first (see the next chapter) rather than grinding one at a time.

The mannequin

The preview model in front of you is not a statue. Poke him and he reacts, and where you poke changes what you get. There are angry zones, sad zones and surprised zones. Poke his feet enough and he gets genuinely sad about it. Nobody has to find this. Everybody does.

Chapter 04

Combat 戦闘

This is the deepest system in the game and the one worth actually learning. Offence is easy. Defence is where the ceiling is.

The chain

Your basic attack is a five-hit chain. The chain has variants you steer into it: hold Space for a launcher, hold S for a slam. The fifth hit ragdolls your victim and sends them flying, which is both the reward for landing a full chain and the moment they get a chance to escape.

Air combos

Launcher, then juggle them in the air, then air slam to put them back on the ground. From a double jump you also get a dive slam. Air routes do not do more damage for free, they do it because staying in the air is harder to escape from.

The heavy

M2 is your heavy. It is deliberately telegraphed with a red glint so a good opponent can see it coming, but it breaks raised guards, which is the answer to someone who will not stop blocking. And it can be feinted, which is the answer to someone who has learned to punish it.

Defence

There are five ways to not get hit, and they cost different things.

OptionHow it works
Block Raise your guard and take chip damage. Your guard meter drains. Empty it and it shatters, and you eat a 2 second stun. Blocking is not free safety, it is a resource.
Perfect block Raise the guard on the exact frames the hit lands and you absorb it completely. Anything. No chip, no meter. This is the highest-value defensive option in the game.
Parry F Runs on bubbles. Here is the important part: a successful parry spends a bubble, a failed attempt does not. You earn bubbles back by landing full combos. Parry has a 5 second cooldown, so it is a scalpel, not a wall.
Dodge Comes with invincibility frames. Cheap, spammable, and the answer to most pressure.
Perfect dodge I-frame directly through a hit rather than around it and you arm a counter punch. Risky read, big payoff.
The pressure law While you are being comboed you cannot attack back. Mashing does nothing. Your only outs are block, dash, aura burst, and tech. Internalise this and half your losses stop happening.

Movement tech

  • Dash cancel. Dash out of your own M1 recovery to stay safe or reset your pressure.
  • Tech. Jump the instant you get launched and you breakfall out of the ragdoll instead of eating the full ride. It works on the mobile jump button too, so phone players are not locked out of it.
  • Wall splat. Launch someone into a wall and they crunch into it and crumple. Positioning is damage.

Aura burst G

You build burst by taking hits, which means the person losing the exchange is the one who gets the escape button. Burst detonates you out of a combo. It is not free: it can be parried, and if you whiff it you are wide open. Burst is a read, not a panic button.

Ryu strike

Legendary card unlock Click at the exact instant your punch connects and the next one ignites: 1.5× damage and the screen cracks. Ryu also arms the counter off a perfect block. It is a timing minigame layered on top of your normal chain, and once you can do it consistently your damage ceiling changes shape.

The ambush

Crouch, get behind someone, stay unseen, and you get an assassination: three quarters of their maximum health in one full execution animation. They get magnetised in, arched back and crumpled. It is the single most violent thing in the game.

The counter exists: the En sense card can auto-slip an ambush before it lands. Zetsu on the attacker's side fuels it. This is a real cat-and-mouse layer, not a gotcha.

Chapter 05

Nen 念

Nen is gated behind the Hunter Exam. There is no shortcut and there is no code for it.

Awakening

  • You must be a Hunter. Pass the exam first.
  • You awaken it by meditating.
  • You cannot meditate in safe zones. The world wants you exposed for this.
  • Try to meditate before the exam and all you get is a whisper: "something feels weird..."

The six principles

Each one trains independently, five levels each. Their sum is your nen rank.

纏TENYour baseline aura. The foundation everything else sits on.
練RENOutput. Pushing your aura harder than it wants to go.
絶ZETSUShut it all off. You vanish, and you take +30% damage.
凝GYOFocus. Concentrate aura where you need it to be.
円ENSense. Feel what you cannot see coming.
硬KOEverything into one punch. Shatters guards.

Zetsu is a trade, not a stealth button

Zetsu hides you and it is what makes the assassination possible, but while you are in it you take 30% more damage. If you get caught in zetsu you are not going to like what happens.

Nen rank

Add up your six principle levels and you get a rank on the F → SSS ladder. You wear it on your shoulder, where everyone can read it before the fight starts. It also feeds your Total Power.

Chapter 06

Nen cards 念札

Every principle level-up deals you three cards. You keep one. Forever.

Common

The backbone of most builds. Solid, stackable, unglamorous.

Rare

Build-shaping. Where your character starts to be a specific character.

Legendary

Roughly one hand in thirty contains one. These change how you play, not how hard you hit.

There are around 56 cards in the pool. Two you will hear people talk about:

  • RYU — THE FLOW unlocks the Ryu strike and the perfect-block counter.
  • SIXTH SENSE is the En card that can auto-slip an incoming assassination.
Deck thinking Because a level-up only ever gives you one keep out of three, your card set is a record of the choices you made, not a shopping list you filled. Two players with identical stats can play completely differently.
Chapter 07

Stats & Total Power 能力

Four stats, a hard cap, a soft cap that bites before it, and one permanent decision at the end.

STRcap 50
DEFcap 50
AGIcap 50
STYLEcap 50

Soft cap sits at 40. Points past it still count, they just stop feeling generous.

What AGI actually does

Do not read AGI as "run faster". It is your stamina tank, your stamina regen, and a discount on what everything costs. An AGI build does not out-run you, it out-lasts you and gets to use its options more often than you get to use yours.

Two different worlds: PvE and PvP

AgainstHow your stats read
NPCs Full power. A 50 STR build hits for roughly +27%. Grinding shows.
Players Measured in notches: about 0.2% per 10 points. That is deliberate. It means a fresh player and a maxed player can have a real fight and skill decides it.
Players at max level The notches quintuple. Once everyone is capped, the build finally matters again.

The uncap

Permanent. No respec. At max level you choose one of two paths, and you live with it:
  • ALL IN — one stat to 70.
  • SPLIT — two stats to 60.

Total Power

TP is the one number you flex. It folds together your level, your spent points, your nen rank, your awakening and your style tier into a single figure, and it shows up in both Stats and Settings. It is the fastest way to know whether the person walking toward you is a problem.

Chapter 08

Fighting styles 流派

You do not buy a style. You take it off a master by beating him.

Karate is live. Boxing, Muay Thai and Kickboxing have their slots waiting. You learn by sparring the master, and there are ten difficulty rungs. You climb them one at a time, in order.

RungWhat you are up against
LV 1A breeze. He is teaching, not fighting.
LV 2–6He stops being polite. Real routes, real punishes.
LV 7–9ENRAGE phases. Glowing smashes that cannot be parried. Your parry habits stop working here.
LV 10THE TRUE MASTER. 2×+ your stats and a genius AI: he reads dodges, punishes whiffs, anti-airs you, and remembers your parry timings.
Style XP is meant to hurt You only earn style XP from wins. Losses give nothing. And there is a 30 minute cooldown after a win. This is scarcity on purpose: a high style tier is a statement about how many real fights you have won, not how many hours you left the game open.
Chapter 09

The living island 島

Whale Island runs whether or not anyone is looking at it.

Bandits with opinions

  • Camps rise on their own. Clear one and it comes back.
  • They have morale. Break it and they rout, running rather than dying.
  • Leaders call for reinforcements, and they arrive.
  • Some of them hit enrage phases when things go badly for them.
  • They react to your INFAMY. Rookies dread a famous killer and keep their distance. Veterans do the opposite: they come hunting.

Down an enemy and you can grip and carry the body, with executions available from there.

The sky

Day and night cycle, and the moon walks through nine hand-drawn phases. One of them is the RED MOON. If you see it, that night is different.

Seasons, weather, and the climate gauge

  • Four seasons turn, though the island is spring about 95% of the time.
  • Rain shows up roughly one day in ten. Winter brings blizzards.
  • A Breath-of-the-Wild style climate gauge tracks how hot or cold you are.
  • Push it and the screen answers: hand-drawn frost and heat veils, blue or red skin tints, shivering in the cold, heavy breathing in the heat.

Chapter 10

Life & economy 暮らし

Jenny ¥

One currency. It comes in from bandit bounties and fishing, and it goes out on spar fees and spins. If you are broke, go hunt something.

Titles

Twenty titles to earn and wear. They are the other half of your identity, next to your nen rank and your Total Power.

Gangs and crews

Form a crew, and your crew has its own infamy board. Reputation on this island is a group sport if you want it to be.

Emotes

Press + for the wheel. The icons are chalk-drawn faces: angry, ow, sad, wowis, question. They are not stickers. Each one runs real head choreography with its own VFX and voice.

Sleep and the legend

  • Stand still for 2 minutes and your character falls asleep. Snoring included.
  • There is a 40 minute idle animation in the game. It is a legend, and almost nobody has seen it.
  • Here is the detail that matters: the timer keeps running while you are asleep. Being asleep does not reset it.
Go find it Forty minutes. One animation. If you have seen it, you know.
