The island

Whale Island does not wait for you

A port town, a forest, a shore, and a sky spar arena. Above them a moon that moves through nine hand-made phases, including the red one. Four seasons turn, blizzards come in winter, rain rolls in about one day in ten, and the climate gauge answers all of it. Get cold enough and you shiver. Get hot enough and you start breathing hard.

The bandits are not decoration. Camps rebuild themselves, leaders call for help, morale breaks and whole groups rout. Build enough infamy and rookies start running from you while veterans come looking.

The crown jewel

Combat that answers back

Five-hit chains with launcher and slam variants, air juggles, a heavy that breaks guards and can be feinted. On the other side: block, perfect block, parry on a bubble economy, dodge with i-frames, a perfect dodge that arms a counter, and an aura burst you earn by taking punishment.

While you are being comboed you cannot swing back. Block, dash, burst, or tech. That is the law, and learning the outs is the whole skill ceiling.
